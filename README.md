How to Write an SEO-Friendly Blog Post

A simple guide to optimizing your blog posts for search engines, based on a standard JSON structure.
1. title
This is your most important SEO element.
Include Keyword: Place your main target keyword at the beginning.
Keep it Short: Stay under 60 characters to avoid it being cut off in search results.
Make it Clickable: Use numbers, questions, or strong adjectives.
Example: "10 Quick and Healthy Breakfast Ideas"


2. slug (The URL)
This is the user-friendly and SEO-friendly URL for your post.
Short & Sweet: Use 3-5 words from your title.
Keyword-Rich: Must include your main keyword.
Use Hyphens: Separate words with hyphens (-).
Example: "healthy-breakfast-ideas"


3. content
The body of your post. Write for humans first!
Structure: Use headings (<h2>, <h3>) to organize your content.
Readability: Use short paragraphs, bold text, and bullet points.
Keywords: Mention your main keyword in the first 100 words and a few times naturally throughout.
Internal Links: Add links to other relevant posts on your own website.


4. image
Images help with engagement and can rank in Google Images.
File Name: Name your image file with descriptive keywords before uploading (e.g., avocado-toast-breakfast.jpg).
Alt Text: Always add descriptive "alt text" to your images. This helps search engines understand what the image is about.


5. category & tags
These help organize your site and show search engines what your content is about.
category (1 per post): The broad topic your post falls under.
Example: "Nutrition"
tags (2-4 per post): Specific, micro-topics covered in the post.
Example: ["breakfast", "quick-recipes", "healthy-eating"]


6. author & date
These build trust and signal freshness.
author: Use a real name to build authority and trust (Google E-E-A-T).
date: Keep your content fresh. Update older posts and change the date to show they are current.
